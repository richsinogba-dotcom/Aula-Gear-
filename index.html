What makes it different

Every detail, deliberate.

We didn't tune one spec and call it done. We rebuilt the whole stack so typing feels effortless and sounds right.

Chassis

CNC-milled 6063 aluminum

A single block of aerospace-grade aluminum, machined to ±0.05mm tolerance. Zero flex, perfect weight, a damped, controlled feel on every keystroke.

Switches

Hot-swappable, 5-pin

Swap switches in seconds. Ship with linear Nebula switches; compatible with every MX-style switch on the market.

Mount

Gasket mount

Porous foam + silicone gaskets between plate and case give a soft bottom-out without mushiness.

Lighting

Per-key RGB with sidelight

16.8M colors per key plus an edge diffuser that wraps the desk in ambient light. Syncs with your setup over USB or stays offline.

Performance

8000 Hz, 0.2ms

True 8K polling over USB. Wireless mode holds 1000 Hz on a 2.4 GHz dongle with a 3-month battery life.

Keycaps

PBT double-shot, OEM profile

Thick PBT that won't shine. Legends that never fade. OEM profile tuned for long coding sessions.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really hot-swappable?

Yes — 5-pin hot-swap sockets, no soldering. Works with any MX-compatible switch.

Does QMK/VIA work out of the box?

Yes. Full QMK support and a VIA JSON ships with the board. Remap keys in real time.

How loud is it?

Graphite finish ships with poron case foam. Measured ~48 dB at 30cm. Optional PE foam available.

Wireless battery life?

~90 days at typical usage with RGB at 30% brightness. ~12 days with RGB at max.

International shipping & warranty?

Ships to 40+ countries. 2-year warranty, global, parts included.
